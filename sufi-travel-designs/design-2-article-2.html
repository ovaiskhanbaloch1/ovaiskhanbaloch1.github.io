Hajj & Umrah: When to Book for the Best Rates

Hajj & Umrah - Sufi Travel Blog

← Back to Blog

Pilgrimage flights and hotels near the Haram sell out early. Here is how early booking protects both your budget and your peace of mind.

Umrah demand rises sharply in Ramadan and during school holidays, and Hajj places are limited every year.

Booking early gives you first choice of hotels near the sacred sites in Makkah and Madinah, better flight times, and fares before peak pricing begins.

It also leaves time to arrange visas, vaccinations and travel protection properly.
