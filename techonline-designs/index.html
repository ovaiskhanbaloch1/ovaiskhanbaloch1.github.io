TECH ONLINE — Design Demos

Six updated designs for the new Tech Online store. Design numbers match the PDF shared earlier. Open any design and browse all of its pages — every page works.

What is new in this update: every design now has a different layout for every page and its own animation style. Every home page has a hero image slider with arrows. Search works (try a product name, a category or a brand), shop filters are dropdowns that really filter, every blog card opens a full blog article page, and checkout has a clean payment section — Cash on Delivery, Bank Transfer, Online Payment (JazzCash / EasyPaisa) and Order on WhatsApp — placing an order sends the full order details to the store WhatsApp. All designs are fully responsive on mobile with a hamburger menu.
