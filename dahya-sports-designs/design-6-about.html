Technology

How Your Kit Is Made

We make sports uniforms for all sports teams. Whatever your sport, we design a unique design for your team members with custom names, numbers and sizes for every player.

Technology

Sublimation

World-class sublimation techniques give your uniforms a shining look. Your design is printed on a roll of paper with sublimation ink, then the printed paper and fabric are fused under high temperature so the ink is vaporised onto the fabric.

Technology

Cut & Sew

We meet every kind of cut & sew requirement, whether you need a few sample pieces or a large production run. Our pattern makers build a perfect pattern from your artwork, picture or even a rough sketch, and original patterns are customised to your sizes.

Technology

Screen Printing

Customised screen printing on team uniforms, school uniforms, t-shirts, fleece hoodies, polo shirts, cheerleading garments and all other sportswear. Player names, school names, team names and numbers are screen printed, with a quick turnaround.

Technology

Embroidery

Embroidery applique is a nice way to add branding to your team uniforms. From 1 colour to multicolour logos and decorative designs, on team uniforms, school uniforms, t-shirts, polo shirts and hoodies. Thread colours are matched accurately to your logo; metallic and glitter threads are available.
